# Two country budgets with shared categories

Decided: 2026-10-31

The money lives in two countries and two currencies, and each side is planned in its own currency. So there's a US budget in USD and a Germany budget in EUR. Categories are shared by both, and each month's amounts are set per country and category. A transaction has no country of its own: it follows its account's country, so a purchase on a US account always counts against the US budget. Moving a purchase between country budgets isn't supported. The Combined view adds the two together in USD and can't be edited.
